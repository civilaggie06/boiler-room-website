The Lounge

Underground, by design.

Boiler Room takes its name from its home — a below-street level space in the historic York Rite Building at 311 W 7th Street, one of downtown Austin's oldest addresses. The result is a hideaway that feels removed from the noise above: a mix of seating, warm brass fixtures, and the slow curl of pipe smoke.

This is a lounge built around the ritual, not the rush. Whether you're breaking in a new pipe or working through a private humidor selection, the room is yours to slow down in.

Boiler Room is a bring-your-own lounge: bring your own cigars or pipe tobacco, and bring your own beverages. We supply the space, the seating, and — for members — the locker.

Open to the public Thursday through Saturday, and available around the clock to members through private locker access.
